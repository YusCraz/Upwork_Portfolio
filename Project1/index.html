FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you service?

We serve Your City and the surrounding communities listed in our service area section. If you don't see your neighborhood, contact us and we'll let you know.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Every estimate is free. We review your property, talk through your goals and give you a clear written quote.

Do you offer recurring lawn maintenance?

Yes. We offer weekly and bi-weekly maintenance plans that include mowing, edging, blowing and basic cleanup.

Can you renovate existing flowerbeds?

Absolutely. Through our FlowerbedsandGardens service we clean out, redesign, replant and re-mulch existing beds.

Do you provide seasonal cleanups?

Yes. We offer spring and fall cleanups including leaf removal, bed cleanup, cutting back perennials and debris haul-away.

Can I request multiple services together?

Of course. Many customers combine lawn maintenance, bed upkeep and shrub trimming into one plan. Just note it in your estimate request.

How do I schedule service?

Request an estimate online, call or text us. Once you approve your quote, we'll confirm a date that works for you.
